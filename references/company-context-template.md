# Company context (template)

Copy this file to `references/company-context.md` and fill it in. Cartographer reads it first on every run, so keep it short: compressed facts, not the full ICP deck. Update it when the ICP changes, and let the skill refresh the previous-map inventory after each run (it will show you the diff first).

## The company in three sentences

What you sell, to whom, and why now. Include the positioning line you want channels to carry and anything you concede to competitors.

## ICP firmographics

Geography, stage, size (revenue, headcount), team shape that signals fit, budget signals, verticals.
